Hi! I am a PhD student at [UC San Diego](https://math.ucsd.edu/), advised by [Claus Sorensen](https://sites.google.com/view/csorensen). 

I am broadly interested in number theory and representation theory, particularly mod-\\(p\\) representations of \\(p\\)-adic groups and their connections to the \\(p\\)-adic and mod-\\(p\\) Langlands program. More specifically, I study admissibility and duality in the derived category of smooth representations, as well as its relationship with differential graded Hecke algebras. I am also interested in mod-\\(p\\) Satake transforms and their role in the study of smooth representations of \\(p\\)-adic groups.
