Hi! I am a PhD student in the math department at [UC San Diego](https://math.ucsd.edu/). My advisor is [Claus Sorensen](https://sites.google.com/view/csorensen). I am broadly interested in number theory and representation theory, particularly mod-\\(p\\) representations of \\(p\\)-adic groups and their connections to the \\(p\\)-adic and mod-\\(p\\) Langlands programs. More specifically, I study admissibility and duality in the derived category of smooth representations, as well as its relationship with differential graded Hecke algebras. I am also interested in higher mod-\\(p\\) Satake transforms and their role in the study of smooth representations of \\(p\\)-adic groups.
